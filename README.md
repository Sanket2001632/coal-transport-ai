\# 🚛 Coal Transport AI

\## Intelligent Delay Prediction \& Operational Risk Management



An end-to-end Machine Learning application that predicts coal transportation delays and converts model predictions into actionable operational risk levels.



The project combines Machine Learning, feature engineering, business risk optimization, and an interactive Streamlit dashboard to help transportation teams identify high-risk trips before delays occur.



\---



\## 🎯 Project Objective



Coal transportation operations can be affected by:



\- Heavy rain

\- Traffic congestion

\- Low vehicle speed

\- Long transportation distances

\- Loading and unloading delays

\- Previous trip delays

\- Driver experience

\- Vehicle fuel efficiency

\- Safety conditions



The objective of this project is to build an AI-powered system that can:



1\. Predict whether a trip is likely to be delayed.

2\. Estimate the probability of delay.

3\. Classify trips into operational risk levels.

4\. Identify major risk factors.

5\. Recommend operational actions.

6\. Provide an interactive management dashboard.
